Garlic Chicken Stir Fry

Description

A quick, colorful stir fry with tender chicken, crisp vegetables, and a savory-sweet garlic sauce. Ready in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams boneless chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, sliced thin
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoons fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoons honey
  • 0.3 cups water
  • 2 green onions, chopped

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken: In a bowl, toss 500 grams boneless chicken breast, sliced thin with 2 tablespoons soy sauce and 1 tablespoons cornstarch. Let sit for 10 minutes to marinate.
  2. Make the sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoons oyster sauce, 1 tablespoons honey, and 0.3 cups water. Set aside.
  3. Cook the chicken: Heat 1 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden and cooked through, about 5 minutes. Remove and set aside.
  4. Stir-fry the vegetables: Add the remaining 1 tablespoons vegetable oil to the pan. Add 1 red bell pepper, sliced, 1 cups broccoli florets, and 1 carrot, sliced thin, and stir-fry until crisp-tender, about 4 minutes.
  5. Add aromatics: Add 3 garlic cloves, minced and 1 teaspoons fresh ginger, grated, and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  6. Combine and glaze: Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss everything together until glossy and heated through, about 2 minutes.
  7. Garnish and serve: Garnish with 2 green onions, chopped and serve immediately.
